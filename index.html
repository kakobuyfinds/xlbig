How to order through HipoBuy

  1. Open a CAFANS listing, check that your size and color are available, then look for HipoBuy under Buy Link.
  2. Compare measurements. For shoes, check whether the size chart gives foot length or insole length. For tops, compare chest width and body length with a garment that fits you, using the same measuring method.
  3. If warehouse quality-control photos are available, compare the received color, size label and visible condition with the listing. Ask for a measurement photo if the size is uncertain, and check the seller’s return window before approving shipment.
  4. Compare shipping quotes for your destination using the final parcel dimensions. A bulky box can raise the billable weight even when the item is light.

Estimate your delivered cost

Use the same currency for all amounts. Include domestic delivery and agent charges under fees if they apply. Add taxes or duties there if they are due separately.

HipoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HipoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product directory for finding and comparing items you may want to order through the HipoBuy shopping agent. The listings on this page open on CAFANS, where you can check each item and its buying options.

Do the listed prices include international shipping?

The amounts shown are estimated item prices. Check your shipping quote and any service charges, taxes or duties for your destination before adding up the order.

What should I check if a listing is unavailable?

Try searching CAFANS for the model or item name. Compare the exact variant, measurements and seller terms before choosing a replacement.
